0.1 Finance kya hai?

Simple definitionFinance ka matlab hai: paisa aaj aur paisa kal ke beech ka khel, jisme risk bhi hai. Koi aaj paisa lagata hai, kal usse zyada milne ki umeed mein. Ye umeed pakki nahi hoti. Bas itna hi hai.

Isliye finance ke do sawaal hamesha aate hain:

Sawaal 1: Kitna milega?
Future mein kitna paisa aane ki umeed hai (cash flow).
Sawaal 2: Kitna pakka hai?
Wo paisa sach mein aayega ya nahi (uncertainty / risk).

Slide 1A ki pehli hi baat yahi hai: asset ki value in dono cheezon se tay hoti hai.

0.2 Time value of money: aaj ka Rs 100 kal ke Rs 100 se zyada kyun hai

Maan lo tumhare paas aaj Rs 100 hain. Tum bank mein 5% interest pe rakh do to ek saal baad Rs 105 milenge. Matlab Rs 100 aaj = Rs 105 ek saal baad.

Ek saal baad ki raqam = aaj ki raqam x (1 + i)
Aaj ki raqam = ek saal baad ki raqam / (1 + i)

Yahan i = interest rate (ek saal ka). Doosri line hi sabse zyada kaam aayegi. Isse pata chalta hai ki future ka paisa aaj kitne ka hai.

ExampleKoi bole "ek saal baad Rs 10,000 dunga". Agar interest rate 5% hai to ye Rs 10,000 / 1.05 = Rs 9,523.81 ke barabar hai aaj. Agar interest rate 12% hai to Rs 10,000 / 1.12 = Rs 8,928.57. Interest rate jitna zyada, future ka paisa aaj utna kam kaam ka.
Ye baat yaad rakhoInterest rate upar jata hai to future ke paise ki aaj ki value neeche aati hai. Isi se aage chal ke "bond ka price aur interest rate ulte chalte hain" wala result nikalega.

0.3 Asset, security, cash flow

  • Asset: koi bhi cheez jo value rakhti hai aur future mein fayda de sakti hai. Ghar, painting, sona, share, bond.
  • Real asset: dikhne wala. Ghar, painting.
  • Financial asset / security: ek kaagaz (ya electronic record) jo batata hai ki tumhe future mein paisa milega. Share aur bond isi category ke hain.
  • Cash flow: asset se aane wala paisa (rent, dividend, interest, ya bechne ka paisa).

0.4 Return, risk aur diversification

Return = (jo mila - jo lagaya) / jo lagaya

Example: Rs 9,000 lagaye, saal baad Rs 10,000 mile. Return = 1,000 / 9,000 = 11.1%. Slide 1A ke bond example mein exactly yahi hai.

RiskRisk ka matlab hai ki result pakka nahi hai. Kabhi zyada mil sakta hai, kabhi kam, kabhi kuch nahi. Zyada risk wali cheez ke liye log zyada return maangte hain, warna koi lega hi nahi.

Diversification = saare ande ek tokri mein mat rakho. Agar tumne 10 alag companies ke share liye hain aur ek company ki factory jal gayi, to sirf 10% nuksan. Ye wala risk diversify ho jata hai. Lekin agar poori economy hi mandi mein chali gayi, to saari tokriyan ek saath girengi. Is wale risk ko diversify nahi kar sakte. Ye hi slide mein idiosyncratic aur systematic risk ka fark hai (Part 1 mein aayega).

0.5 Liquidity aur leverage

Liquidity
Kisi asset ko kitni jaldi aur bina nuksan ke cash mein badal sakte ho. Bank deposit liquid hai. Ghar illiquid hai, bechne mein mahine lagte hain. Jaldi bechna pade to sasta bechna padta hai (fire sale).
Leverage
Udhaar lekar invest karna. Jitna zyada udhaar apni poonji ke muqable, utna zyada leverage. Isse fayda bhi bada hota hai aur nuksan bhi.

1.3 Bonds

BondEk security jo fixed amount ek tay time pe dene ka vaada karti hai. Matlab tum company ya government ko udhaar dete ho, aur wo vaada karte hain ki itne paise itne time pe wapas denge.

Bond ka price aur interest rate (slide ka main derivation)

Maan lo ek bond hai jo ek saal baad Rs 10,000 dega. Aaj uska price P hai. Agar tum aaj P dekar kharidte ho aur ek saal rakhte ho, to:

Return = (10,000 - P) / P
Isi ko bond ka interest rate i kehte hain:
i = (10,000 - P) / P
Slide ka exampleAgar P = 9,000 to i = 1,000 / 9,000 = 0.111 = 11.1% per year.

Ab isse P nikalte hain (algebra step by step):

i = (10,000 - P) / P
i x P = 10,000 - P
i x P + P = 10,000
P (1 + i) = 10,000
P = 10,000 / (1 + i)

Ye wahi formula hai jo Part 0 mein tha. Matlab bond ka price = us future payment ki aaj ki value.

  • Corporate bond: company doob sakti hai, to default ka risk hota hai.

1.5 Risk: systematic vs idiosyncratic

Bond aur stock dono ki value earnings ki uncertainty pe depend karti hai. Slide risk ko do type mein todti hai:

Systematic risk
Wo events jo bahut saari financial assets ko ek saath hit karte hain. Diversify nahi ho sakta (undiversifiable).
Examples: trade policy mein badlav, interest rate mein badlav, poori economy mein demand ka girna.
Idiosyncratic risk
Wo events jo sirf ek firm ya ek asset ko hit karte hain. Diversify ho jata hai, isliye valuation mein irrelevant.
Examples: company ka naya product successful hona, ya company ka koi lawsuit haar jana.

Required rate of return

  • Jis company mein systematic risk zyada hai, uske share ki earnings zyada hilti-dulti hain, aur ye hilna-dulna diversify nahi hota.
  • Isliye investor aise shares pe zyada average return maangte hain.
  • Wo return jo investor ko share kharidne ke liye convince kare, usse required rate of return ya market capitalization rate kehte hain.
  • Systematic risk zyada, to ye rate zyada.
  • Conclusion (slide ka): same expected earnings ho to jis company ka market capitalization rate kam, uske share ki value zyada.
Extra intuition (slides mein formula nahi hai, bas idea ke liye) Maan lo ek company se har saal Rs 100 ki earnings aane ki umeed hai, hamesha ke liye. Ek simple approximation:
Value = Expected earnings / Required rate of return
Required rate 10% to value = 100 / 0.10 = Rs 1,000. Required rate 20% (zyada systematic risk) to value = 100 / 0.20 = Rs 500.
Earnings same hain, sirf risk zyada hai, aur value aadhi. Isi ko slide "lower market capitalization rate, higher value" kehti hai. Ye formula sirf samajhne ke liye hai, exam mein slides ki language use karo.

1.6 Trading strategies

Ab tak humne ye dekha ki asset ki fundamental value kaise tay hoti hai. Lekin log sirf fundamental value dekh ke trade nahi karte.

Strategy 1: Fundamental value pe
Jo asset apni fundamental value se sasta lag raha hai usse kharido, jo mehenga lag raha hai usse becho. Active mutual funds aur kuch hedge funds ye karte hain. Soch ye hai ki price jaldi hi fundamental value pe wapas aayega.
Strategy 2: Momentum
Fundamental value dekhte hi nahi. Bas ye dekhte hain ki price kidhar ja raha hai. Badh raha hai to kharido (aur badhega), gir raha hai to becho (aur girega).
Momentum ka exampleEk share ki fundamental value Rs 100 hai, lekin price Rs 120 chal raha hai aur roz badh raha hai. Momentum trader Rs 125 mein bhi khareed lega, kyunki use lagta hai ki kal Rs 130 hoga. Wo fundamental value se zyada de raha hai, lekin bech ke profit ban jayega, agar price sach mein badhta raha.
SpeculationDono strategies speculation hain. Pehli mein belief ye hai ki price jaldi fundamentals pe wapas aayega. Doosri mein belief ye hai ki short-run trend aage bhi chalega. Ye saari strategies aaj ke markets mein hain, aur wahi prices ka behaviour aur bubbles/crashes ki possibility tay karti hain.

1.8 Continuous double auction aur order book

Asli stock market mein "ek ghante ke time period" jaisa kuch nahi hota. Trade continuously hota hai aur price hamesha badalta rehta hai. Is mechanism ko continuous double auction kehte hain. "Double" isliye ki buyers aur sellers dono bid/offer lagate hain.

Limit orderKoi bhi khareedne ya bechne wala ek price aur quantity ka combination submit kar sakta hai. Ye limit order hai.
Limit buy order: "Main 1,000 RIL shares Rs 1,162 ya usse kam price pe khareedunga." Ye buyer ka reservation price hai (isse upar nahi dega).
Limit sell order: "Main itne shares bechunga, par price is amount se kam nahi." Ye seller ka reservation price hai.

1.9 Asset market bubbles

Share prices sirf ghante-ghante ya din-din mein hi nahi hilte. Kabhi kabhi bahut bade swing bhi aate hain. Slide ka example: Nasdaq Composite Index, 1995 se 2004. Ye index kai stocks ke prices ka weighted average hai, aur us time isme bahut saari tez badhne wali, value karne mein mushkil technology companies thi.

Slide ke numbers aur unka calculation
  • 5 saal mein 750 se 5,000+ ho gaya. Annual rate = (5000/750)^(1/5) - 1 = ~46%. Slide kehti hai "around 45%".
  • Phir ek saal se kam mein do-tihai value kho di.
  • Bottom ~1,100 pe, peak se lagbhag 80% neeche (1 - 1,100/5,000 = 78%).
  • Is episode ko tech bubble kehte hain.
Bubble ki definition (slide)Kisi bhi asset (financial ya koi aur) ke price ka uski fundamental value se lamba aur bada gap ban jana.

Prices mein news kaise aati hai

Kabhi kabhi asset ki fundamental value ki nayi information market mein jaldi aur sahi tareeke se price mein aa jaati hai. Firm ki future earnings growth ki beliefs badalte hi share price lagbhag turant adjust hota hai. Patents ya lawsuits ki acchi/buri news, important logon ki bimari ya jaana, earnings surprises, mergers aur acquisitions, ye sab active trading aur tez price movement laate hain. Ye normal aur healthy hai.

Bubble kaise banta hai

Slide ka core argumentPrice ke movement mein firm ki financial health ke baare mein information hoti hai. Jinke paas wo information nahi hai, wo price movement dekh ke use samajhne ki koshish karte hain. Yani price changes messages hain. Agar market theek chalna hai to traders ko in messages pe react karna padega. Lekin jab wo price badhne ko aur badhne ka sign samajhne lagte hain (momentum strategy), to cycle khud ko mazboot karne lagti hai.

Bubbles ke liye 3 favourable features

1. Resale value
Asset mein price ke badalne pe speculation ka mauka hai. Jaise ghar, painting.
2. Ease of trading
Financial markets mein khareedar se vikreta aur wapas banna bahut aasaan hai. Isse speculative trading badhti hai.
3. Ease of borrowing
Agar log udhaar lekar apni demand badha sakein, to price ka upar jaana jaari reh sakta hai.

Kya bubbles sach mein hote hain? Fama vs Shiller

Slide ka sawaal: kya bubble ek real cheez hai, ya sirf hindsight (peeche mudke dekhne) ka illusion? Kya crash se pehle jaana ja sakta hai ki market bubble mein hai?

Eugene Fama ka view
  • "Bubble" word ka koi matlab nahi, bas fashion hai. Crash ke baad kehna aasaan hai "wo bubble thi".
  • Hamesha koi na koi kehta rehta hai ki prices zyada hain. Jab wo sahi nikalte hain to log unhe sar pe bithate hain, galat nikalte hain to bhool jaate hain. Wo lagbhag aadhi baar sahi, aadhi baar galat hote hain.
  • Tech bubble mein prices us waqt ki available best information pe based the. Prices isliye hile kyunki companies ke prospects ki information tezi se badal rahi thi.
Robert Shiller ka view
  • Simple, publicly visible statistics (jaise price-to-earnings ratio) se bubbles ko banta hua pehchana ja sakta hai.
  • "Leaning against the wind": jo asset cheap hai usse kharido, jo mehenga hai usse becho. Short run mein loss ho sakta hai, lekin long run mein diversified portfolio se zyada gain.
  • 1990s ke late mein prices isliye badhe ki log expect karte the ki price aur badhega. Ye irrational exuberance thi.
Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH)Fundamental values ke baare mein saari generally available information prices mein lagbhag turant shamil ho jaati hai.
Slide ka quote: agar koi economist ke paas aisa formula hota jo ek hafte pehle crisis predict kar sake, to wo formula bhi generally available information ban jata aur prices ek hafte pehle hi gir jaate. Matlab crisis ko predict karna possible hi nahi (is hypothesis ke hisaab se).
Counter-argument (slide ka)Practice mein har investor ko ye nahi pata hota ki baaki investors bubble ke against trade kab shuru karenge. Is uncertainty ki wajah se koi bhi akela investor bubble ke khilaf lagne se ghabrata hai, kyunki ho sakta hai bubble aur lambi chale aur wo pehle hi kat jaye. Isliye investors bubble ke saath chalna hi pasand karte hain, aur price correction ek lambi der ke baad achanak hota hai.

2.2 Financial intermediaries (banks) kyun toote

Financial intermediary = jo savers aur borrowers ke beech ka kaam karta hai (bank sabse bada example). Slide kehti hai ki 3 cheezein milke risk badhati hain:

1. High leverage
Apni poonji kam, udhaar bahut zyada.
2. Illiquid assets

Banks itne leveraged kyun the? (slide ke 3 reasons)

  1. Unhone risk ko underestimate kiya.
  2. Managers ko high expected returns wale projects lene ka incentive tha (zyada return, zyada bonus).
  3. Banks ne structured investment vehicles (SIVs) ke zariye regulation se bachna sikh liya.

SIV kya hai

  • Liability side pe SIV ne investors se short-term debt liya.
  • Asset side pe SIV ne alag alag type ki securities rakhi.
  • Bank ne guarantee di thi ki zarurat padi to wo SIV ko funds degi.
  • Isse banks leverage ke regulatory requirements se bach pa rahe the.
  • Jab ghar ke prices gire aur mortgage loans pe default badhe, to SIVs aur unhe back karne wale banks ki solvency pe sawaal uthe (solvency = kya unke assets unki liabilities se zyada hain).

  • Agar bank sirf local loans deta hai, to us shehar ke ghar ke prices girne pe bank khud musibat mein aa jata hai.
  • Agar bank ke portfolio mein mortgages alag alag jagah ke hon, to ek jagah ki giravat se bachna possible hai (diversification).
  • Aisi hi ek product thi mortgage-backed security.
Securitization ke side effects (slide) Banks ye dhyan dena kam kar dene lage ki borrower sach mein loan chuka payega ya nahi (kyunki loan to bech dena hai). Aur rating agencies ne aise securities ki value ko galat assess kiya. Dono ne milke risk badha diya.

Jab ghar ke prices girne lage, to investors in securities ko hold karne se ya SIVs ko udhaar dene se ghabrane lage. Banks aur SIVs ke bahut saare assets illiquid ho gaye: unhe assess karna aur bechna dono bahut mushkil tha, bas fire sale prices (bahut sasti keemat) pe hi bik sakte the.

  • SIVs ki poori funding aisi hi thi.
  • Checkable deposits (aam logon ke bank deposits) deposit insurance se protected the, lekin baaki investors nahi.
  • Jab investors ko SIVs ke assets ki value pe shak hua, to unhone apna paisa wapas maangna shuru kar diya.

Structured finance: risky cheez ko "safe" kaise banaya gaya

3.1 Rise and fall

  • Global Financial Crisis ke pehle ki sabse hairan karne wali kahaniyon mein se ek: structured finance products ka tezi se chadhna aur girna.
  • 10 saal se kam mein US mein in products ka issuance dus guna se zyada badh gaya.
Structured financeEconomic assets (loans, bonds, mortgages) ko ek pool mein ikattha karna, aur phir us pool ke against prioritized claims issue karna. Un claims ko tranches kehte hain.
Prioritization ki wajah se bahut saari banayi hui tranches pool ke average asset se kaafi safe hoti hain.
Core idea ek line meinRisky collateral ko repackage karke usme se "safe" assets nikalna. Isi se bahut zyada "safe" securities banin aur investors ko virtually risk-free lagi (rating agencies ne bhi aisa hi certify kiya). 2008 ke crisis ke core mein ye discovery thi ki ye securities originally advertise kiye gaye risk se kaafi zyada risky thi.

Do sawaal jo poori story ko explain karte hain

  1. Rating ki fragility: securities ko high rating tabhi mil sakti hai jab rating agencies underlying securities ke default risk aur defaults ke correlated hone ka sahi estimate lagayein. Parameters mein chhoti si galti se tranche ka default risk itna badal sakta hai ki AAA tranche bhi reasonable probability se default kar jaye.
  2. Systematic risk ka substitute: securitization wo risk jo bade hisse mein diversifiable tha, use highly systematic risk se badal deta hai. To ye securities kisi severe economic downturn mein survive karne ke chance mein same rating ke traditional corporate securities se bahut kam hain. Investors ko aisi claims ke liye same rating ke corporate bond se kaafi zyada risk premium maangna chahiye tha.

Dono features (rating ki nazakat aur systematic risk ka exposure) mil ke structured finance ke spectacular rise aur fall ko samjhate hain. Inka detail aage aayega.

3.2 Credit rating agencies

  • Moody's, Standard & Poor's, Fitch: 100 saal se zyada se ye agencies financial, industry aur economic information analyse karke alag alag entities ki creditworthiness (loan chukane ki kabiliyat) ka independent assessment deti hain. Isse rating scales bane: AAA, AA, A, BBB aur aage.
  • Historically unka zyada kaam single-name corporate finance tha, matlab aisi cheezein jinki creditworthiness ek hi company se judi ho.
  • Crisis se pehle ke saalon mein unka business model us se aage badh kar structured finance tak phail gaya.
Origin story: structured securities "rated" market kyun bane
  • Shuru se ye market "rated" market tha, jisme tranches ka risk credit rating agencies assess karti thi.
  • Issuers chahte the ki naye products bhi bonds wali scale pe rate ho, taki wo investors bhi khareed saken jo ratings-based constraints mein hain (jaise koi fund jise sirf investment grade bonds rakhne ki ijazat hai).
  • Rating milne se issuers ne ek illusion of comparability banaya: ye naye securities purane "single-name" securities jaise lagne lage.
  • Isse ek bade pool ke buyers mil gaye un products ke liye jo warna bahut complex derivative securities maane jaate.

3.3 AAA-rated securities kaise "banaye" jaate hain

GoalKisi bhi given credit rating wali security banane ke liye uska cashflow risk rating agencies ke guidelines ke hisaab se tailor karna padta hai. Cashflow risk = default ki probability + default hone par loss kitna hoga. Structured finance ye do steps se karta hai: Pooling aur Tranching.

ResultAlag alag risk wali securities (kuch AAA bhi)

Step 1: Pooling aur SPV

  • Bahut saare credit-sensitive assets ek portfolio mein jama kiye jaate hain. Ise typically special purpose vehicle (SPV) kehte hain.
  • SPV originator ki balance sheet se alag hota hai. Taki uski liabilities (tranches) ka credit risk originator ki balance sheet se isolate ho jaye.
OriginatorWo bank ya institution jisne original loans diye (ya assets banaye) aur ab unhe pool mein daal raha hai.

Pass-through securitization (jisme tranching nahi hai)

Maan lo SPV ne prioritized nahi, simple fractional claims issue kiye, matlab har claim underlying portfolio ke payoff ka ek barabar hissa. Isse pass-through securitization kehte hain.

  • Portfolio ka expected loss = underlying securities ke mean expected loss ke barabar.
  • To portfolio ki rating = pool ke securities ki average rating.
  • Claims ko wahi rating mil jaati hai. Koi credit enhancement nahi hua.

Step 2: Tranching (asli jadoo yahan hai)

Alag alag cashflow risk wali securities banane ke liye ek capital structure of prioritized claims issue hota hai, jise tranches kehte hain. Tranches isme alag hoti hain ki loss kaun pehle absorb karega.

Subordination / overcollateralizationSenior tranche tabhi loss uthata hai jab junior claims (uske neeche wali) khatam ho chuki hon. Isse senior tranche ko pool ki average rating se zyada rating mil sakti hai. Junior claims jo protection deti hain, use overcollateralization kehte hain, aur ye senior tranche ki rating tay karne mein bahut important hai, kyunki ye tay karta hai ki senior claim impair hone se pehle portfolio ka sabse bada loss kitna sah sakta hai.

"Diversifiable risk ko systematic risk se badalna" ka matlab

Part 1 ka systematic/idiosyncratic risk yaad karo. Ek AAA corporate bond ka default risk zyada tar us ek company ke apne problems se aata hai (idiosyncratic, diversifiable). Lekin senior tranche tabhi default karti hai jab pool ke bahut saare assets ek saath default karein. Aisa tab hota hai jab poori economy buri haalat mein ho (jaise ghar ke prices girna). Corporate AAA bond: 1% matlab "kisi saal is ek company ke saath kuch bura ho jaye". Baaki duniya theek chal rahi hai, tumhari naukri, ghar, doosre investments sab theek.
Senior tranche: 1% matlab "poori economy ka bura crash". Us time tumhari naukri, ghar, aur baaki investments bhi gir rahe hote hain. Paisa tab sabse zyada chahiye hota hai, aur wahi time tranche default karti hai.

Structured finance ko rate karna itna mushkil kyun tha

Part 3 mein dekha ki tranching se safe claims ban sakti hain. Ab sawaal ye hai: rating agencies ne un claims ko rate kaise kiya, aur wo itni galat kyun nikli? Is part mein numbers zyada hain, lekin logic simple hai. Ek ek karke chalte hain.

4.1 Credit rating kya hoti hai

Credit ratingIssuer ya entity ki apni future financial commitments (principal aur interest ki payments) poori karne ki kabiliyat ka measure.

Rating do tareeke se base ho sakti hai:

1. Default ki probability
Default hone ki anticipated likelihood.
2. Expected economic loss
Default hone ki likelihood x default ke baad severity of loss.
Expected loss = P(default) x Loss given default (LGD)
LGD = 1 - Recovery rate
ExampleBond ki default probability 5%, aur default hone par 50% paisa recover hota hai (recovery 50%, to LGD 50%). Expected loss = 5% x 50% = 2.5% of face value.

Intuitively rating ko security ke expected cash flow ka measure samajh sakte ho.

Investment grade vs speculative grade (corporate bonds) BBB ya usse upar (AAA, AA, A, BBB) = investment grade. Low se moderate default risk.
BB+ aur neeche = speculative grade. Ye already default mein hain ya default ke kareeb.

  • Rating jitni neeche, default probability utni tezi se badhti hai. AAA ki 10 saal ki probability 0.19% hai, BBB- ki 7.26%, aur C ki 95%.

4.3 Structured finance ko rate karna alag kyun hai

Single-name rating (pehle ka business)
Har security ko doosre se independent assess kiya jaata hai. Rating agency ko ye sochne ki zarurat nahi ki ek company ka default doosre se kitna jura hua hai (correlation ke baare mein agnostic reh sakti hai).
Structured finance rating
Agency ko poore collateral pool ke payoffs ki joint distribution samajhni padti hai: matlab kaun kitna ek saath default karega. CDO tranches ka risk defaults ki commonality ke prati sensitive hai.
Magnification (multiple rounds ka asar)
  • CDO ka structure default likelihoods, default correlation ke estimates ki imprecision, aur default dependence ke model errors ka effect magnify kar deta hai.
  • Ye problems tab aur badhti hain jab alag alag financial securities ko mila diya jata hai (jaise CDO-squared mein).
  • Multiple rounds of structuring mein underlying securities mein itni chhoti galtiyan, jo us security ki apni rating nahi badaltin, structured securities ki ratings ko dramatically badal sakti hain.

4.7 Real world: mortgage-backed CDOs kaise CDO-squared the

  • Pichle ek dashak mein bahut saare CDOs ke underlying assets subprime residential mortgage-backed securities (RMBS) the.
  • In RMBS mein se bahut saare khud bade mortgage pools ki tranches the, jo pehle round ke securitization se bani thi.
  • To "CDO of mortgage-backed securities" = effectively CDO-squared (tranches ka CDO).
  • Subprime lending recent phenomenon tha, to is sector ki defaults aur delinquencies ka historical data kam tha.
  • Default correlations, default probabilities aur recovery rates ke estimates mein galti ki sambhavna badi thi.
  • Ye galtiyan re-securitization se magnify ho gayi, aur isi ne in securities ke devastating losses samjhaye.

Problem 1: Bond ka interest rate aur price

Ek bond ek saal baad Rs 10,000 dega. (a) Agar iska price Rs 9,200 hai to interest rate kya hai? (b) Agar market mein similar assets ka rate badhkar 10% ho jaye, to bond ka price kitna hona chahiye? (c) Price badha ya gira, aur kyun?

Answer dekho (a) i = (10,000 - 9,200) / 9,200 = 800 / 9,200 = 8.70%.
(b) P = 10,000 / (1 + i) = 10,000 / 1.10 = Rs 9,090.91.
(c) Price 9,200 se 9,090.91 par gira (lagbhag Rs 109 ki giravat). Wajah: bond ki payment fixed (Rs 10,000) hai, aur investor ko ab 8.70% ki jagah 10% return chahiye. Return badhane ka ek hi tareeka hai ki price kam ho.
Problem 2: Risky vs safe bond

Do bonds dono ek saal baad Rs 5,000 dene ka vaada karte hain. Government bond Rs 4,750 mein bikta hai, ek company ka bond Rs 4,400 mein. (a) Dono ka promised interest rate nikalo. (b) Company bond ka price kam kyun hai? (c) Agar company default kar jaye aur kuch na de, to investor ka return kya hoga?

Answer dekho (a) Government: (5,000 - 4,750) / 4,750 = 250 / 4,750 = 5.26%. Company: (5,000 - 4,400) / 4,400 = 600 / 4,400 = 13.64%.
(b) Company ke default ka risk zyada hai. Isliye investor zyada rate (13.64%) maangte hain, aur rate zyada hone ka matlab price kam. Same promised payment par riskier bond ka price kam hota hai.
(c) Default aur zero payment par return = (0 - 4,400) / 4,400 = -100%. Promised rate zyada thi, lekin default hone par saara paisa gaya.
Problem 3: Systematic vs idiosyncratic (identify karo)

Neeche diye gaye events ko systematic ya idiosyncratic risk mein classify karo: (i) RBI ne repo rate badhaya. (ii) Ek pharma company ka naya drug approve ho gaya. (iii) Government ne imports pe naya tax lagaya. (iv) Ek retail company ki CFO ne resign kiya. (v) Poori economy mein demand 5% gir gayi.

Answer dekho (i) Systematic (interest rates sab assets ko affect karte hain). (ii) Idiosyncratic. (iii) Systematic (trade policy). (iv) Idiosyncratic. (v) Systematic.
Trick: poochho ki "ye ek saath bahut saari companies ko hilata hai?" Haan to systematic, nahi to idiosyncratic.

Problem 8: Do-bond tranching (p = 20%)

Do identical bonds, har ek ki default probability 20%. Ek senior aur ek junior tranche (har ek 1 ki). (a) Defaults uncorrelated hon to dono tranches ki default probability? (b) Default correlation 0.5 ho to? (c) Correlation 1 ho to?

Answer dekho (a) Dono survive = 0.8 x 0.8 = 0.64. Junior default = 1 - 0.64 = 36%. Senior default (dono default) = 0.2 x 0.2 = 4%.
(b) P(dono default) = p^2 + rho p(1-p) = 0.04 + 0.5 x 0.2 x 0.8 = 0.04 + 0.08 = 0.12. Senior = 12%. Junior = 2p - 0.12 = 0.40 - 0.12 = 28%.
(c) P(dono) = 0.04 + 1 x 0.16 = 0.20. Senior = 20%, Junior = 0.40 - 0.20 = 20%. Dono bond ke jaisa, koi credit enhancement nahi.
Ek aur observation: correlation 0 se 1 jaate hue senior 4% se 20% ho gayi (bura hua), junior 36% se 20% (accha hua).
Problem 9: Teen-bond tranching (p = 20%, uncorrelated)

Teen identical bonds, har ek p = 20%. Teen tranches: junior (koi bhi 1 default), mezzanine (2 ya zyada default), senior (teeno default). Teeno ki default probability nikalo aur batao kitne tranches bond ke 20% se kam risky hain.

Answer dekho k defaults ki probability = C(3,k) x 0.2^k x 0.8^(3-k):
k=0: 0.8^3 = 0.512
k=1: 3 x 0.2 x 0.64 = 0.384
k=2: 3 x 0.04 x 0.8 = 0.096
k=3: 0.2^3 = 0.008
Junior = 1 - 0.512 = 48.8%. Mezzanine = 0.096 + 0.008 = 10.4%. Senior = 0.8%.
Bond ka 20% hai. Mezzanine (10.4%) aur senior (0.8%) dono kam risky hain, junior (48.8%) zyada. Teen mein se do tranches kam risky, yaani capital ka 2/3 hissa. (Wahi pattern jo slide ke p = 10% wale example mein tha.)
Problem 10: CDO-squared (p = 20%)

Do alag, independent two-bond pools (har bond p = 20%). Har pool ki junior tranche leke ek naya pool bana, aur uske do tranches (senior aur junior) issue kiye. Naye senior aur junior ki default probability?

Answer dekho Pehle: har pool ki junior ka default = 36% (problem 8a).
Senior^2 tab default karti hai jab dono junior default karein: 0.36 x 0.36 = 12.96%.
Junior^2 tab default karti hai jab koi ek junior default kare: 1 - (1 - 0.36)^2 = 1 - 0.4096 = 59.04%.
36% default wali claims se humne 12.96% wali claim banayi, jo ek bond ke 20% se bhi kam hai. Lekin ye tabhi tak sach hai jab dono pools independent hon.
Problem 11: Tranche loss (attachment points 0-6-12)

100 bonds ka pool, recovery rate 40%. 15 bonds default ho gaye. Junior (0-6%), mezzanine (6-12%) aur senior (12%+) ko kitna loss hoga?

Answer dekho LGD = 1 - 0.40 = 0.60. Ek bond ka loss pool ka 0.6%. 15 x 0.6% = 9% pool loss.
Junior: pura 6% gaya = 100% loss.
Mezzanine: (9 - 6) = 3% loss, size 6%, to 3/6 = 50% loss.
Senior: loss 12% tak nahi pahunchta, to 0 loss.

Problem 13: Short answer (theory)
Q13a. Bubble ki definition do aur batao ki Fama aur Shiller ke views mein kya farak hai. Bubble = kisi asset ke price ka uski fundamental value se sustained aur significant departure. Fama kehte hain ki "bubble" word ka koi matlab nahi, ye hindsight hai, aur prices hamesha available information reflect karte hain (EMH). Shiller kehte hain ki simple statistics (jaise P/E) se bubble pehchani ja sakti hai, aur 1990s ke prices irrational exuberance se chadhe the.
Q13b. Securitization se banks ka risk kam hone ki umeed thi. Phir bhi crisis kyun aaya? Diversification se banks local housing risk se bach sakte the (MBS ke through alag jagah ke mortgages). Lekin: banks ne borrower ki repayment capacity dekhna kam kar diya, rating agencies ne risk galat assess kiya, banks ne SIVs ke through leverage badhaya, short-term wholesale funding pe nirbhar hue, aur jab prices gire to assets illiquid hokar firesale prices pe bike.
Q13c. Structured finance ke "do neglected features" kya hain? (1) Ratings underlying risks ke estimates mein chhoti imprecision ke prati bahut fragile hain (AAA bhi reasonable probability se default kar sakti hai). (2) Ye diversifiable risk ko systematic risk se badal deta hai, to ye securities severe downturn mein same rating ke corporate securities se kam survive karti hain, aur investors ko iske liye bahut zyada risk premium maangna chahiye.
Q13d. Zero lower bound ka matlab aur Fed ne kya kiya? Policy rate ko zero se neeche nahi le ja sakte. December 2008 tak Fed ne rate zero kar diya, to monetary policy constrained ho gayi. Fed ko unconventional monetary policy apnani padi, aur sarkar ne fiscal policy (tax cuts aur spending badhana) use ki.

6.1 Formulas ek jagah

Bond rate: i = (F - P) / P (F = future payment, P = price)
Bond price: P = F / (1 + i)
Return: (final - initial) / initial
Expected loss: P(default) x LGD, LGD = 1 - recovery
Joint default: P(both) = p^2 + rho p (1 - p) (2 bonds, same p)
Two-bond: Senior = P(both), Junior = 2p - P(both)
Three-bond uncorrelated: Junior = 1-(1-p)^3, Mezz = P(k>=2), Senior = p^3
CDO-squared (2 pools): Senior^2 = Junior^2, Junior^2 = 1-(1-Junior)^2
Tranche loss: Junior = min(L, 6)/6, Mezz = min(max(L-6,0), 6)/6, Senior = max(L-12,0)/88 (L = pool loss in %)
Annualized -> n-year PD: 1 - (1 - annual)^n

6.4 Exam mein ye galtiyan mat karna

Common mistakes
  • Bond ka price aur interest rate ulte chalte hain (rate badhe to price gire). Seedha mat likh dena.
  • Risky bond ka promised rate zyada hota hai, actual return nahi. Default ho gaya to nuksan.
  • Idiosyncratic risk diversify hota hai, isliye valuation mein irrelevant. Systematic nahi hota.
  • Limit order ka matlab "market price pe" nahi hota. Wo sirf us price ya usse behtar pe hoga.
  • Buy order turant fill ho to ask price pe hota hai, sell order bid price pe.
  • Senior tranche ko loss sabse baad mein lagta hai. Junior ko sabse pehle.
  • Correlation badhe to senior ka risk badhta hai (junior ka kam hota hai).
  • CDO-squared = tranches ka CDO (CDO ki tranches ko dobara pool karna). CDO of mortgage-backed securities bhi effectively yahi hai.
  • Monetary policy (central bank) aur fiscal policy (sarkar) ko mix mat karo.
